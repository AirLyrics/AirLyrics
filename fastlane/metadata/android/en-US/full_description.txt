AirLyrics is a lightweight floating lyrics app.

Features:

• Detect current media and show synced floating lyrics
• Search for lyrics online through NetEase Cloud Music or Musixmatch
• Import local standard LRC or word-by-word lyrics, display translations, and adjust timing
• Customize the floating window and switch between light and dark themes

AirLyrics is not a music player and does not include a complete lyrics database. If an online match is inaccurate, you can import local lyrics.

Privacy:

AirLyrics does not collect or share personal data. Notification access is used only on-device for media detection; online search sends only the song information required to find lyrics.

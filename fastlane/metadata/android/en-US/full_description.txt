AirLyrics is a lightweight floating lyrics app.

Features:

• Detect current media and show synced floating lyrics
• Search NetEase Cloud Music, Musixmatch, LRCLIB, Spicy Lyrics, or Apple Music (sign-in required), and preview results before applying them
• Import local LRC or TTML files, including LDDC word-timed LRC; display translations and adjust timing
• Edit saved lyrics and export, import, or delete them in batches
• Customize the floating window and switch between light and dark themes

AirLyrics is not a music player and does not include a complete lyrics database. If an online match is inaccurate, you can import local lyrics.

Privacy:

AirLyrics has no analytics or own server. Notification access is used on-device for media detection. Online searches go directly to the selected providers; optional Apple Music sign-in sends an account token to Apple.
